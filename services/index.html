What we do

What we do, and how we do it.

Pre-construction estimating, interior production painting, exterior coatings, and specialty finishes, all self-performed on the same buildings for the same clients. One contractor. One schedule. One phone number.

01 · Pre-construction

Estimating that ends the scope argument before it starts.

The number you sign is the number you pay, and everyone on the job is looking at the same drawing.

We break the spec down by substrate, product, and sheen, then produce color-coded finish prints. The field builds from them. Your PM checks against them. When a question comes up on floor twelve, everyone is looking at the same drawing.

We have bought from Sherwin-Williams since the day we opened in 2013. Pricing gets locked with the manufacturer at bid and held for the life of the contract, so your number survives the months between award and mobilization.

  • Specification takeoff by substrate, product, and sheen
  • Color-coded finish prints for the field and for your PM
  • Material pricing locked with the manufacturer at bid
  • Submittals, samples, and mock-ups where the spec calls for them

02 · Interior

Interior production painting

Built for buildings with hundreds of identical units and one turnover date that is not moving.

  • Residential units: walls, ceilings, doors, frames, trim, closets
  • Corridors, stairs, and elevator lobbies
  • Amenity spaces, leasing offices, clubhouses
  • Back-of-house, mechanical, storage, and parking levels
  • Level 5 and specialty finishes where specified
  • Every sheen the spec calls for: flat, matte, eggshell, semi-gloss, and high sheen, in standard or custom colors
  • Wallpaper and wallcovering in amenity and common areas
  • Punch and turnover touch-up through final inspection

Drywall repair stays limited to what painting needs. Wallcovering gets scoped in writing, so there is nothing to argue about at punch.

03 · Exterior

Exterior painting and coatings

Done on buildings we are already inside, so you are not managing a second painter.

  • Exterior doors, frames, and railings
  • Balconies, soffits, canopies, and site elements
  • Plaster, stucco, fiber cement, masonry, and metal substrates
  • Every sheen from flat to high gloss, standard or custom color
  • Exterior-grade and industrial coatings as specified

04 · Specialty

Specialty finishes and coatings

The finishes that make a lobby, an amenity floor, or a parking level look like somebody cared. One contractor, one schedule.

  • Epoxy floors and sealants
  • Venetian plaster and decorative plaster finishes
  • Limewash and lime slurry finishes
  • Marking paint and line coating: parking levels, columns, and safety striping
  • Wall murals and graphic wall finishes
  • Specialty paints and custom colors
  • Wallpaper and wallcovering
  • Industrial coatings as specified

The MPA Way

Same sequence. Every floor. Every building.

The MPA Way is not a slogan. It is how every painter and crew leader we put on your job is trained to work. Do one building with us and the next one feels exactly the same.

01

Kickoff and prep

Before a single can is opened, our PM sits down with your team. Scope. Sequence. What "ready" looks like. How touch-ups get handled. Then the walls get prepped and checked against the finish schedule.

02

Primer, sprayed

Sprayed for speed and even coverage. This is the base that makes everything after it last.

03

Finish coats, by hand

Hand-applied. Crisp lines. Consistent color. A finish that stands up to punch, and to the people moving in.

04

Three walks

The crew leader walks it every day. Our PM walks it several times a week, and again before anyone says "done." Then you walk it with us. Nothing is turned over until you sign.

05

Turnover

Punch closed. Client walk signed. Closeout paperwork in your hands. On to the next floor.
