About

A painting company operating to meet today's demands: proactive, prepared, and professional.

Antonakos Painting & Contracting, Inc., doing business as MPA Painters, opened in Elizabeth, New Jersey in July 2013. We are the painting subcontractor to builders and general contractors on new construction across New Jersey, New York, and Pennsylvania, all of it run from one headquarters in Elizabeth.

History

Thirteen years, one direction.

Started by two painters who wanted a company built on integrity, hard work, and showing up, for clients and for crews.

2013

Antonakos Painting & Contracting incorporated in New Jersey. Michael Antonakos and Hector Martinez Mejia open in Elizabeth. First Sherwin-Williams account.

2017

Bidding high-rise packages with regional builders. Mid- and high-rise multifamily becomes the core of the business.

2020

Industry-wide supply disruption. MPA's supplier planning keeps material on hand and projects on schedule while much of the market waits.

2022

Operational restructure. W-2 field workforce, internal pay scale, documented HR and compliance, Maureen Poole joins as Director of Operations.

2023

Operations consolidated into the Elizabeth headquarters. Written safety program formalized with a dedicated safety consultant.

2026

Twenty-five active contracts across the tri-state area, 50+ W-2 employees, and an 18 to 24 month contracted backlog.
